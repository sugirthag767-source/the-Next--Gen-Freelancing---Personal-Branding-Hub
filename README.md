# FORGE — Dominate the Creative Field

A landing page built to feel like a creative studio, not just another course website.

## What it is?

FORGE is a one-page site for a course on video editing, social growth, freelancing, and AI creative tools. It's designed to feel alive — things move, glow, and respond as you scroll.

## What makes it special

- A glowing 3D shape spins gently behind the hero, reacting to your mouse
- The headline types itself in, word by word
- A photo slider glides through real work, non-stop
- Every section fades and lifts into view as you scroll
- The reviews section is the star — testimonials fall in and land with a soft splash, like raindrops
- Each part of the page has its own mood: bright and electric for reviews, deep and gold-toned for the curriculum
- Cards tilt gently when you hover, like they're following you
- A friendly bonus popup appears right when it's most useful

## The feeling

Dark backgrounds, bright glowing colors, smooth motion. Built to feel premium, exciting, and easy to trust — the same feeling the course promises to teach.

*A concept build, made to show what's possible on a single scroll.*
